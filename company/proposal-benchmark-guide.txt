PROPOSAL BENCHMARK GUIDE

The user supplied these two past proposals as the template/benchmark for Draft proposal:
- basis/IFB - Portable Restroom Trailer Rental_Temporary 123.pdf (Denver, IFB 11213).
- basis/Proposal - Proposal for Temporary Living Quarters, Showers, and Heads .pdf (USCG).

Use the current solicitation and its amendments to decide the required format, page limits,
sections, forms, CLINs, submission method, and deadline. These benchmarks show presentation
and depth only. Statements and instructions in them relate to their original procurements.

For restroom and shelter sanitation proposals, use the Denver benchmark's organization:
cover/title information, required cover sheet and addenda acknowledgment, table of contents,
executive summary, company overview, technical response and scope compliance, operations
and staffing, health and safety, environmental/sustainability response, past performance,
and required forms/certifications. Include a price proposal using the current bid schedule.
Use only sections and forms relevant to the current solicitation.

Its technical response covers delivery and deployment; site access and placement; trailer
configurations, accessibility, climate control and winterization; utilities; cleaning and
sanitization; pump-outs and lawful waste disposal; consumables; emergency repair, escalation
and replacement; staffing and dispatch; training, PPE, spill response and sharps awareness;
and environmental practices. Address these topics when relevant, using confirmed current
facts or explicit placeholders. Do not reuse the Denver 48-hour cleanout promise, fleet
configurations, capacity, chemicals, or local forms as established facts for another bid.

For living quarters and combined shower/head proposals, use the USCG benchmark's structure:
cover letter, contents, company overview and core information, technical approach with
pre-deployment/delivery/operations/demobilization phases, compliance and safety, utilities,
operational readiness, equipment specifications, price table, past performance, required
documentation. Adapt section numbering continuously to the current proposal.

Match the benchmarks' complete, professional narrative and useful tables. Avoid generic
claims and repetition. Reference the actual scope, agency, site, quantities, and performance
period throughout. Do not target 15 or 34 pages simply because the examples have that length.
Markdown drafts should have a contents list without invented page numbers.

Use company/profile.md and capability-statement.txt for company facts; preserve their CONFIRM
flags. Do not infer a successful award from a historical proposal. Do not copy old contacts,
recipient salutations, dates, signatures, unit counts, prices, certificate declarations,
FAR/HSAR clauses, or Denver forms into another procurement. Historical pricing can serve
only as a labeled reference; mark every proposed price [CONFIRM PRICE].

Some original pages contain embedded charts, equipment photographs, or scanned forms not
represented by the extracted text. Use the original PDFs when those details are needed.
List required forms as outstanding until the actual current forms are prepared and reviewed.
Never claim that an unsigned or unverified certification has been executed.

End with '## Before you submit': each placeholder and confirmation, every current submission
requirement with its source, required attachments, submission method, and exact deadline.
