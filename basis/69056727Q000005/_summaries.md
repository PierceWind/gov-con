## 69056727Q000005_Amend 0002.pdf

Posted 2026-11-06

Amendment 0002 to the Federal Highway Administration Western Federal Lands Division’s Request for Quotation (RFQ) 69056727Q000005 adds an organized site visit requirement and extends the quote submission deadline for modular building and restroom rental with ancillary services.

The amendment, issued November 6, 2026, adds Section D, paragraph 3.1, scheduling the site visit for Tuesday, November 17, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. Pacific time at the WFLHD Main Entrance, 610 East Fifth Street, Vancouver, Washington. Attendees must provide company, attendee, and contact information, arrive at least 10 minutes early, and comply with federal facility access and REAL ID requirements.

The quote deadline is extended to Tuesday, December 8, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. Pacific time. The technical-question deadline is December 1, 2026, at 4:00 p.m. Pacific time.

All other solicitation terms remain unchanged, and offerors must acknowledge receipt of the amendment.

The underlying unrestricted, full-and-open RFQ seeks a firm-fixed-price task order for an anticipated 24-month period from approximately February 1, 2027, through January 31, 2029, at WFLHD’s secure facility in Vancouver, Washington.

The contractor must lease and operate:

- One approximately 24-by-60-foot, 1,440-square-foot prefabricated office building;
- Two 8-by-40-foot finished office container or modular units; and
- One climate-controlled executive restroom trailer with at least three stations.

All units must be turnkey and operational by March 1, 2027. The primary office must contain at least three offices, one secure meeting room, and two conference rooms.

The office units must meet commercial-code, ADA, HVAC, electrical, flooring, lighting, security, and Cat6 telecommunications requirements.

Required ancillary services include:

- Delivery;
- Site preparation;
- Leveling, blocking, anchoring, and skirting;
- OSHA steps and ADA ramps;
- Utility connections and certification;
- Post-installation re-leveling within 30–60 days;
- Quarterly HVAC and utility preventive maintenance;
- Warranty-backed technical support;
- Weekly septic pumping;
- Freshwater replenishment;
- Restroom supply stocking;
- Winterization; and
- Assignment of an account manager.

At lease completion, the contractor must demobilize within 30 calendar days, remove all units and access systems, disconnect utilities, perform final restroom pumping and sanitization, transport the assets away, and restore the sites to clean, vacant, level condition.

The procurement uses NAICS 532490, with a $40 million size standard, and PSC W054.

## 69056727Q000005 QandA_2026-11-03.pdf

Posted 2026-11-05

This Vendor Questions and Answers document issued for Solicitation No. 69056727Q000005 addresses the Modular Buildings & Restroom Rental with Ancillary Services requirement, clarifying eligibility, subcontracting, experience, ownership, deadlines, site visits, and past-performance evaluation for the solicitation.

The acquisition is confirmed as an unrestricted, full-and-open, open-market procurement rather than one limited to holders of a GSA Multiple Award Schedule contract under MAS SIN 532490P. Responsible vendors registered in SAM.gov may submit quotations even if they do not hold a GSA Schedule contract; the solicitation’s contrary reference was identified as a typo and will be corrected through Amendment 0001.

The requirement is for a new contract, and there is no mandatory site visit. Vendors are instructed to review the FAR and all posted RFQ attachments.

Offerors may subcontract modular-unit supply, installation, utility services, and recurring restroom services. For Factor 2C, Relevant Past Experience/Proof of Experience, an offeror may use the relevant experience of proposed subcontractors or major suppliers, including the firm providing and servicing the modular units, and must submit that subcontractor experience for evaluation.

Subcontractor past performance alone may not be used to satisfy Factor 3; past-performance information for both the prime contractor and subcontractor is required. The leased modular buildings and restroom trailers remain titled to the contractor because the Government is leasing rather than purchasing the units, so no title transfer is required.

The questions deadline and Past Performance Questionnaire subject-line discrepancies were typographical errors to be corrected in Amendment 0001. A response dated November 3, 2026, addressed whether an offeror without relevant past performance would receive a neutral Factor 3 rating or could submit key-personnel past performance.

## 69056727Q000005 QandA_2026-11-04.pdf

Posted 2026-11-05

This Vendor Questions and Answers document issued for Solicitation No. 69056727Q000005 addresses the requirement for modular buildings, restroom trailer rentals, and ancillary services, clarifying solicitation eligibility, proposal submission, subcontracting, evaluation factors, leasing terms, deadlines, and related procurement procedures.

The acquisition is an unrestricted, full-and-open, open-market requirement—not limited to GSA Multiple Award Schedule holders. Responsible vendors registered in SAM.gov may submit quotations even if they do not hold a GSA Schedule contract. The reference to MAS SIN 532490P in Section E is identified as a typo to be corrected through Amendment 0001. The requirement is a new contract, and partial quotations, including quotes limited solely to restroom services, will not be accepted.

The agency confirmed that offerors may subcontract the supply, installation, utilities, and recurring servicing of modular units and restroom facilities. For Factor 2C, relevant experience requirements may be satisfied using proposed subcontractor or major supplier experience, including the firm providing and servicing the modular units, and supporting documentation would be required for evaluation.

However, subcontractor performance alone may not be used for Factor 3 past-performance evaluation. Past-performance information must be provided for both the prime contractor and subcontractor.

The contractor will retain title to leased modular buildings and restroom trailers because the arrangement is a lease rather than a purchase, so no title transfer is required.

No mandatory site visit is scheduled, although vendors should review the FAR and all posted RFQ attachments. The solicitation’s question deadline and inconsistent Past Performance Questionnaire subject lines contain typographical errors that will be corrected in Amendment 0001.

Vendors may view the publicly available interested-vendor list attached to the SAM.gov RFQ only after registering and signing in to SAM.gov. There is no separate organized site-visit attendee list or networking event.

## 69056727Q000005_rfq.pdf

This **Request for Quotation (RFQ)** issued by the **Federal Highway Administration’s Western Federal Lands Highway Division (WFLHD)** solicits firm-fixed-price quotes for **modular building and restroom rental with professional installation, maintenance, technical support, teardown, demobilization, and site restoration**.

Solicitation 69056727Q000005 is an unrestricted, full-and-open commercial acquisition under FAR Part 12, using NAICS 532490 (Commercial and Industrial Machinery and Equipment Rental and Leasing, with a $40 million size standard) and PSC W054. The anticipated performance period is **February 1, 2027, through January 31, 2029**, with the modular-unit lease and monthly support scheduled for **22 months from March 1, 2027, through December 30, 2028**.

The requested pricing consists of four firm-fixed-price line items:

1. **Task 1:** Modular unit lease, 22 months.
2. **Task 2:** Professional installation and ancillary services, one lot, approximately February 1–28, 2027.
3. **Task 3:** Monthly maintenance and lease technical support, 22 months.
4. **Task 4:** Teardown, demobilization, and site restoration, one lot, approximately January 1–31, 2029.

Offerors must complete the Section B price schedule and provide a grand total. They must meet the minimum equipment and component requirements in the Statement of Work and submit documentation supporting relevant work experience and past performance, including the required work-experience and past-performance materials.

The solicitation includes a proposed **24-foot by 60-foot modular-building floor-plan layout** and a Service Contract Act wage determination.

The solicitation was released **October 28, 2026**. Technical questions are due by **4:00 p.m. Pacific Time on November 19, 2026**, and quotations are due to WFLHD in Vancouver, Washington, by **2:00 p.m. Pacific Time on November 24, 2026**.

The RFQ constitutes the only solicitation, and no separate written solicitation will be issued. Quotations must include:

- A completed Standard Form 18.
- The offeror’s SAM.gov Unique Entity Identifier.
- Section B pricing.
- Required technical and experience documentation.
- Relevant past-performance information.
- Completed representations and certifications.
- Acknowledgment of amendments.
- A current SAM.gov registration and Reps and Certifications report.
