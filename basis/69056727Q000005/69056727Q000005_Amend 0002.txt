SECTION D – STATEMENT OF WORK
1.	BACKGROUND
Western Federal Lands Highway Division (WFLHD), a division of the US Department of
Transportation – Federal Highway Administration, designs and administers, primarily,
road construction projects on Federal Lands in the states of Alaska, Washington, Oregon,
Idaho, Montana, and Wyoming.
2.	OBJECTIVE
The objective of this requirement is to support and maintain WFLHD operations during the
Division Office Facility Renovation by obtaining prefabricated modular office building(s),
single-wide container/modular office units, and an executive restroom trailer on a
temporary lease basis, including professional delivery, site preparation, installation, utility
connection, preventative maintenance, weekly septic pumping maintenance, technical
troubleshooting support, and eventual teardown/demobilization.
3.	LOCATION
The delivery and service location shall be at the WFLHD secure federal facility located at
610 East Fifth Street, Vancouver, Clark County, WA 98661-3801.
The Contractor must coordinate the delivery schedule, truck access, and exact site
placements with the Technical Point of Contact (TPOC) at least 10 business days prior to
on-site delivery. This coordination is critical to ensure that delivery vehicles can access
the compound and that drop zones are clear of obstruction. The Contractor is responsible
for preparing the sites, offloading, setup, and placing/setting all units.

information.

The Contractor shall provide the following supplies and maintenance services:

approximately 24' x 60' (1,440 SQFT) containing a minimum of three offices, one

buildings' distribution panels to the closest WFLHD building electrical distribution
panels.

days following initial occupancy to ensure long-term stability and code compliance.

winterization assistance, technical troubleshooting, and general routine
maintenance of all integrated trailer systems.
