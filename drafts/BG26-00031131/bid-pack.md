### Description

The City of Dallas (the “City”) is soliciting Bids to establish three-year with two, one-year renewal options Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (ID/IQ) service price agreement for the rental and maintenance of portable toilets for use by the general public and City staff at various locations throughout the City. An ID/IQ agreement is established when quantities of goods and/or delivery schedules cannot be determined.  Services will be ordered on an as-needed basis during the term of this contract.

### HigherGov AI summary

Background
 The City of Dallas is issuing a solicitation for bids to establish a three-year Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (ID/IQ) service price agreement for the rental and maintenance of portable toilets. This contract aims to provide services for the general public and City staff at various locations throughout Dallas, with the flexibility of ordering services on an as-needed basis. The goal is to ensure clean and sanitary restroom facilities are available at all times.

Work Details
 The contractor will be responsible for the rental and maintenance of portable toilets, which includes: 
1. Delivery of units within 48 hours of request and removal within 24 hours of notification. Emergency requests must be fulfilled within 2 hours.

2. A mutually agreed cleaning schedule prior to delivery, with cleaning performed between 7:30 a.m. and 3:30 p.m., seven days a week.

3. Regular cleaning tasks include emptying holding tanks, disinfecting surfaces, and ensuring all units are stocked with toilet paper.

4. Specific types of units required include regular portable toilets, ADA-compliant units, handicapped accessible units, comfort station trailers, and special event rentals, each with detailed specifications regarding size, materials, and features such as ventilation and holding tank capacity.

5. The contractor must also manage graffiti abatement within 72 hours of notification and handle any minor repairs within the same timeframe.

Period of Performance
 The contract will commence upon City Council approval and execution, lasting for three years with two optional one-year renewals.

Place of Performance
 Services will be performed throughout various locations in the City of Dallas.

Bidder Requirements
 Bidders must have at least three years of verifiable experience in providing similar services. They are required to submit references demonstrating their capability in managing portable toilet rental and maintenance services. Additionally, bidders must comply with the Developing Regional & Inclusive Vendor Enterprises (DRIVE) Policy with a participation goal of 5.71% for certified small business enterprises (SBEs).
