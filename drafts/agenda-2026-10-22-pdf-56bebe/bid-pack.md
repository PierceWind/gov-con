### Description

End Of The Road Ren Fair requests $20,000 in City sponsorship for its 2027 event at the north side of Barney Meyring Park, with approximately 1,200 attendees. The application lists setup on June 17, 2027, and teardown on June 20, 2027, but its event start/end field instead lists June 19–20, 2026; the event dates therefore require confirmation.

Requested funding covers portable toilets, decorations, cleaning staff, beer and light snacks, performance fees, travel costs for in-state and out-of-state performers, radio and social media advertising, emergency equipment including first aid supplies and fire extinguishers, and miscellaneous event expenses.

The application describes $2,000 in locally purchased supplies for stages, backdrops, and guest activities, $1,200 for performer transportation between Anchorage and Valdez, and restroom rentals typically costing upwards of $1,500. Businesses mentioned in descriptions of recurring or past purchasing are not established as awarded 2027 vendors. This is a City-supported nonprofit purchasing opportunity; the $20,000 sponsorship is not a single contract value.

Summary: The City Council meeting agenda schedules a third 2027 appropriations-setting work session for October 22, 2026, covering sponsored events, economic development, facilities, fleet and infrastructure, information services, and major equipment and technology reserves.

The Council is expected to review and raise objections to related appropriation requests. Work-session minutes will not be transcribed, although audio is available upon request. Supporting materials include the event sponsorship application packet and full major-equipment and technology-reserve schedules.

The proposed 2027 sponsored-event budget is $185,128, an increase of $56,130, or 44%, over the 2026 adopted amount of $128,998. City-event funding is proposed at $52,500, bringing the combined event total to $237,628.

Listed sponsorship requests include:

- $5,000 for Advocates for Victims of Violence’s Women of Distinction Awards Night
- $7,000 for Valdez Adventure Alliance’s Odyssey Off-Trail Race
- $5,000 for the Valdez Fat Bike event
- $40,000 for the Valdez Fly-In and Air Show
- $25,000 for Gold Rush Days
- $26,500 for the Mayor’s Cup
- $13,500 for Mountain Man Hillclimb
- $3,000 for Youth Snow-X
- $20,000 for the End of the Road Renaissance Fair
- Additional requests for the Heart of the Chugach Wilderness Experience and other organizations

The AVV request would support a 200-person October 16, 2027, awards event at the Valdez Civic Center, with all City funds used for locally catered food. The organization must submit expenditure reporting within 90 days after the event.

The economic-development discussion identifies $590,000 in public-accommodations-tax revenue held in Council Contingency until Discover Valdez is active and submits a formal budget. It also identifies a baseline VFDA request of $136,277 following discussion of a separate $5 million capital-investment request, and a $62,500 increase for restructured workforce-development contracts.

Facilities and infrastructure changes include:

- $210,000 for heating utilities
- $54,500 for electricity
- $67,000 for operating supplies
- $27,500 for maintenance contracts
- $50,000 for rural snow removal
- $30,000 for fire hydrants
- $28,500 for vehicle-repair parts
- $19,500 for shop supplies

Solid-waste rates remain unchanged. Operating funding increases by $55,000 for bale bags, household-waste costs rise by $20,000, and vehicle processing receives $15,000.

Water and sewer rates also remain unchanged. The budget includes a combined Utility Fund, a $568,000 utilities budget, and a 2027 operating subsidy of $1,261,312.

The major-equipment plan targets reducing the reserve balance from $7.2 million to $4 million and provides $2,043,107 under the 10-year-average method, including:

- $533,854 to refurbish Fire Tanker 3
- $350,000 for a winter trail groomer
- $75,000 for a Streets fuel truck
- $37,290 for a Community Development pickup
- $34,000 for an IT vehicle
- $22,500 for a harbor pressure washer
- $20,000 for a commercial mower

Information Services funding includes $40,000 each for a fiber study and IT audit, $30,000 for radio support, and $20,000 for a CAD audit. Technology reserves address recurring software licenses, hardware and infrastructure, and lifecycle replacement of systems such as radios, computers, servers, cameras, and software platforms.
