### Description

Contractor shall provide all materials, labor, equipment, tools, permits, taxes, transportation, and fees to provide septic pumping services, and portable toilet and hand-washing station rentals for Hemet-Ryan Air Attack Base (RAAB).

### HigherGov AI summary

Background
 The California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection (CAL FIRE) seeks portable sanitary facilities and septic pumping services for Hemet-Ryan Air Attack Base (RAAB).

Work Details
 - Rent and maintain at least two portable toilets and two handwashing stations. Service both types of units twice weekly, including draining, cleaning, sanitizing, and replenishing supplies. Initially place units near the north sides of the Perry Hall dining facility and Dozer Barn automotive cover. Relocate them as needed, including during fire season, at no additional cost.
- Pump septic tanks at least three times per year when requested, removing approximately 1,500 gallons per service. Respond on site within 48 hours of a service call. CAL FIRE may adjust the service frequency and quantity. The rate sheet estimates 13,500 gallons, but this quantity is not guaranteed.
- Provide a vacuum truck and/or trailer. Transport waste to a certified disposal site and provide disposal records to the county agency and CAL FIRE. Vehicles must meet applicable gross vehicle weight and State and Federal Department of Transportation certification requirements.
- The rate sheet estimates 36 months of toilet and handwashing station rentals and four pickup/drop-offs as needed. Estimated quantities are not guaranteed. CAL FIRE may amend the agreement to increase services at quoted rates.

Period of Performance
 Estimated January 1, 2027, or the date of CAL FIRE approval, whichever is later, through December 31, 2030. Services are scheduled Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., excluding State holidays. Work may not begin before approval and notice to proceed.

Place of Performance
 Hemet-Ryan Air Attack Base (RAAB), Hemet, CA. Portable units are initially designated near the north sides of the Perry Hall dining facility and Dozer Barn automotive cover.

Bidder Requirements
 - The prime bidder must be an individual or firm currently licensed to do business in California. Out-of-state bidders must provide current business-license or incorporation documents showing good standing in their state. California or foreign corporations must be active and in good standing with the California Secretary of State.
- The contractor must hold a current Riverside County Septic Pumper Permit and an active California DMV Motor Carrier Permit. The contractor must maintain a valid California Class A or B driver’s license, as appropriate for the vehicle, with an air-brake endorsement during the agreement.
- The awardee must provide commercial general liability coverage of at least $1 million per occurrence; workers’ compensation with waiver of subrogation in favor of the State and employer’s liability coverage of at least $1 million; and commercial auto coverage of at least $1 million. The State must be named as additional insured for general liability and auto coverage.
- Subcontracting is permitted only for subcontractors listed in the bid declaration. Subcontractors must hold licenses, permits, and certifications required for their work.
- No DVBE participation goal is required. Verified DGS-certified DVBE participation may earn a 1%–5% evaluation incentive. Certified California SB/MB bidders may qualify for a 5% preference. Non-small businesses may qualify by subcontracting at least 25% of the net bid price to certified SBs.
