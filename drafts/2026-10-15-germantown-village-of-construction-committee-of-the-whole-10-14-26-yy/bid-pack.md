### Description

Recreation staff requested a $9,000 increase in recreation other expenses to cover portable toilets and landscape work in parks that are not used athletically. Staff stated that the village has only three indoor bathrooms across nine parks and therefore must outfit most parks with portable toilets.

The same expense category pays for landscape services, including "weed control fertilization and all that." Staff characterized these as services provided for park users rather than revenue-generating activities. Athletic-field maintenance costs were discussed separately, with proposed increases in youth athletic field-use charges intended to cover those costs. The $9,000 is the requested increase, not a stated total contract value.

Summary: The committee of the whole meeting addresses Germantown’s continued review and potential action on the Village’s 2027 operating budget, including library and recreation department requests, while also receiving citizen input on capital spending, borrowing, debt capacity, and long-term financial planning.

The meeting was held October 14, 2026, with a quorum present and public notice provided under Wisconsin Statutes §§19.83 and 19.84. Public comments were generally limited to four minutes per person.

The captions are identified as unofficial YouTube captions rather than official minutes.

Citizen Brian Sparrow urged the Village to reconsider portions of its approved capital budget and reduce reliance on debt.

He cited a proposed $440,000 mower compared with a reportedly similar $150,000 Brookfield purchase. He questioned borrowing for recurring costs such as the Axon service contract, recommended using reserves rather than five-year bonds for five squad cars to avoid approximately $50,000 in interest, and criticized financing a $235,000 sealcoating project through borrowing.

He also questioned the structure of approximately $17 million in borrowing, including an estimated additional $1.2 million in interest. He requested a 5-, 10-, and 20-year debt outlook; analysis of credit-rating and interest-rate effects above the Village’s referenced 62% debt-capacity level; emergency borrowing capacity; and household-specific levy impacts.

The Germantown Library presented its 2027 budget, including approximately $372,000 in Washington County revenue. This represents an increase of $30,000 and approximately 27% of the estimated library budget, based on circulation, expenditures, and service to non-library Washington County residents.

Staffing increases reflect wage-study adjustments for four of five full-time employees, a 3.5% overall increase, and higher health premiums, with no added staff or hours.

The library allocated $10,000 of the new revenue to operating needs, added approximately $6,000 for digital materials and $6,000 for utilities, and reported that operating support remains about $20,000 below pre-cut service levels. County funding of roughly $20,000 annually is expected to continue under the current contract.

Recreation proposed approximately $15,480 in additional part-time wages, primarily for an 11th week of Kids Club in 2027 and retention raises, anticipating at least $40,000 in additional revenue.

Other recreation requests included:

- $5,000 for property insurance.
- A recreation software mobile application with a $5,000 initial cost and approximately $3,000 annual cost.
- $5,000 for playground wood-chip replacement.
- $4,000 for facility expenses associated with using multiple school sites.
