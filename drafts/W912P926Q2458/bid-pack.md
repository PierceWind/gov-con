Work Details
 The contractor will be responsible for the following tasks:

1. **Mobile Office Trailer**: Provide a 24' x 60' trailer with four private offices, including two sets of access stairs and perimeter skirting. The trailer must be delivered fully furnished, electrically connected, and operational before billing begins. Maintenance includes repair or replacement of broken items at no additional cost.

2. **Portable Toilet Facilities**: Supply one handicap-accessible toilet and one standard toilet with weekly pumping and sanitation services, along with hand sanitizer in both units.

3. **Office Furnishings & Workstations**: Furnish commercial-grade furniture for 12 personnel, including ergonomic chairs, filing cabinets, adequate lighting, electrical outlets, and HVAC capacity. A conference room package is also required with seating for up to 12 people and necessary equipment like a monitor and whiteboard.

4. **Kitchen Appliances**: Provide a refrigerator, kitchen break table, microwave oven, and coffee maker.

5. **Electrical Hookup Coordination**: Coordinate with Ameren Missouri for temporary electrical hookups and establish a separate account for utility billing. The contractor is responsible for all physical connections from the meter to the trailer.

6. **Security and Personnel Requirements**: Ensure compliance with installation access control policies and complete mandatory security training within specified timeframes.
