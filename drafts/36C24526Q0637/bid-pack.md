Summary: This Visitor Guide from the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, Veterans Health Administration, and VA Maryland Health Care System provides visitor, patient, service, navigation, and contact information for the Perry Point VA Medical Center in Perry Point, Maryland.

The guide includes campus maps, building and floor directories, floor plans for Buildings 361 and 23H, driving directions, public transportation information, parking guidance, and important telephone numbers.

The facility is smoke-free, visitor restrictions may change during respiratory outbreaks, and photography or video is limited to protect patient, employee, and personally identifiable information.

Shuttle service is available on weekdays between Perry Point, the Baltimore VA Medical Center, and the Cambridge VA Outpatient Clinic by reservation.

The guide identifies key locations such as Admissions, eligibility and enrollment, primary care, pharmacy, radiology, laboratory, MRI, CAT scan, patient advocacy, patient travel, dental, optometry, podiatry, physical and occupational therapy, mental health, community living centers, hospice, and residential treatment programs.

Summary: This **facility floor-plan presentation** provides a detailed wayfinding and departmental-location guide for a **VA medical center**, identifying the floors, entrances, check-in areas, clinical services, administrative offices, patient-support functions, and restricted-access areas throughout the building. The Third Floor includes the GI Lab, Pulmonary services, Release of Information, a multipurpose room, and the 3A and 3B inpatient units.

The Fourth Floor houses 4A and 4B clinics, Cardiology and the Cardiac Cath Lab, EKG, Neurology Service Administration, Prosthetics, Beneficiary Travel, Consumer Relations, and MICU/CICU services and waiting areas.

The Fifth Floor includes surgical and medical service administration, 5A Surgical Clinic, 5B inpatient services, SICU, operating-room waiting, surgical and anesthesiology pre-operative areas, Same Day Surgery check-in, Hemodialysis, and the Vascular Lab.

The Sixth Floor contains Mental Health Administration and Clinic, 6A inpatient and 6B outpatient programs, Substance Abuse services, the Returning Veteran Program Office, Veterans Benefit Counselor, Personnel Security, EEO, Sleep Lab, EEG, EMG, Voluntary Service and Public Affairs, and sixth-floor clinic check-in.

### Baltimore Wage Determination.pdf (posted 2026-11-10)

Summary: This Wage Determination, issued by the U.S. Department of Labor’s Wage and Hour Division under the Service Contract Act, establishes minimum hourly wage rates and fringe-benefit requirements for covered federal service-contract employees in Maryland’s Anne Arundel, Baltimore, Baltimore City, Carroll, Harford, Howard, and Queen Anne’s Counties. Wage Determination No. 2015-4265, Revision 35, was last revised August 22, 2026, and covers a broad range of occupational categories, including administrative and clerical, automotive, food service, general support, health care, information technology, instructional, materials handling, maintenance, protective services, technical, transportation, and miscellaneous positions.

Representative wage rates include Accounting Clerk I at $20.51 per hour, Administrative Assistant at $37.69, Customer Service Representative I at $17.65, General Clerk I at $18.17, Computer Systems Support Specialist at $49.51, System Support Specialist at $49.51, Engineering Technician II at $25.72, Engineering Technician V at $43.61, Registered Nurse I through IV, Firefighter, Guard I and II, Truckdriver—Light at $23.11, and Truckdriver—Heavy or Tractor-Trailer at $28.59.

Other listed positions include Cleaner or janitorial classifications under the general services occupations, Aircraft Mechanic I–III, Electrician, Maintenance, HVAC Mechanic, Plumber, Maintenance, Warehouse Specialist, Medical Assistant, Licensed Practical Nurse, Technical Writer, UXO Technician, Airplane Pilot, and numerous food-service and transportation jobs. Rates are specified in the schedule where provided, while some entries require additional classification or wage-rate review.

All listed occupations receive required fringe benefits, including health and welfare of $5.92 per hour, up to 40 hours per week, or $236.80 weekly/$1,026.13 monthly. For contracts covered by Executive Order 13706, the applicable health-and-welfare amount is $5.42 per hour, and workers must receive one hour of paid sick leave for every 30 hours worked, up to 56 hours annually.

Paid sick leave cannot be credited toward SCA fringe obligations. The determination also provides two weeks of paid vacation after one year, three weeks after eight years, four weeks after 15 years, and at least 11 paid holidays annually.

Additional provisions address Executive Order 13658 minimum wages, computer-professional exemptions, night and Sunday premiums for air-traffic-control and weather-observer positions, hazardous-duty differentials of 8% or 4% for designated ordnance-related work, uniform furnishing and maintenance reimbursements, and the SF-1444 conformance process for classifications not listed in the determination.
