### HigherGov AI summary

Background
 The National Park Service (NPS) is seeking quotes for Portable Toilet Services at ZION National Park. This requirement arises from the need to supplement existing restroom facilities due to the increasing number of visitors, ensuring their health, safety, and enjoyment. The contract will be awarded under commercial procedures, and it is a 100% Total Small Business Set-Aside.

Work Details
 The contractor shall furnish all labor, materials, supplies, and parts necessary to maintain portable toilets in a safe operating condition. Specific tasks include:

- Maintaining units in a clean and sanitary manner;
- Removing graffiti within 24 hours;
- Ensuring units are free of litter;
- Sanitizing interior surfaces with disinfectant;
- Stocking dual toilet paper dispensers and wall-mounted waterless hand soap dispensers;
- Scheduling pumping and servicing to maintain health standards;
- Repairing or replacing damaged units within 24 hours;
- Conducting regular inspections;
- Providing quarterly usage reports to NPS;
- Adhering to OSHA standards;
- Submitting a Safety Plan and Spill Plan for review; and
- Reporting any conditions requiring work outside the contract scope.

Period of Performance
 The period of performance is from January 1, 2027, to December 31, 2027, with four one-year option periods extending through December 31, 2031.

Place of Performance
 ZION National Park, Utah.

Bidder Requirements
 Bidders must be registered as a small business. They must also have an active Unique Entity Identifier (UEI). Additionally, contractors must comply with OSHA regulations and provide proof of liability insurance.

Summary: This **Monthly Price Schedule** from **Zion National Park** addresses **portable toilet servicing requirements for PMIS 350233A–E across FY2027–FY2031**, including monthly service quantities, locations, unit rates, estimated monthly costs, emergency services, and base-plus-option-year totals.

The schedule covers recurring service at East Entrance, East Tunnel, Petting Zoo, Temple, Big Bend, Canyon Junction-1, Canyon Junction-2, Nature Center, Oak Creek, and, in FY2027, Shooting Range. Each monthly estimate is calculated from the number of units, services per week, services per month, and per-unit rate.

The schedule includes FY2027 base-year pricing and option-year schedules for FY2028 through FY2031, although some monthly headings contain apparent year inconsistencies. For example, FY2028 entries are labeled 2023, FY2030 entries are labeled 2025, and FY2031 entries are labeled 2026.

Service frequency varies seasonally:

- January, February, April, May, July, September, October, and December generally use four service occurrences.
- March, June, August, and November generally use five service occurrences.
- Higher seasonal quantities begin in March when shuttle service starts.

Nature Center and Big Bend represent consistently significant requirements, while Canyon Junction-2 is included beginning in FY2028 and replaces the FY2027 Shooting Range line.

Temple receives increased servicing in April and October, marked for septic cleaning. Temple is also specifically added in January and February for anticipated parking-monitor operations.

Oak Creek is listed as active throughout the year but may be used at another location if needed. The schedule also identifies red-line items as “if needed only.”

1. INTRODUCTION: 

The National Park Service has a need to place and service temporary toilet facilities in high visitor use areas to supplement existing restroom facilities. The contractor shall furnish all labor, materials, supplies, and parts to maintain portable toilets in a safe operating condition and in accordance with the specifications outlined in this Statement of Work (SOW).

### MASTER_Q_A_0002.xlsx (posted 2026-11-09)

Summary: This Questions and Answers document issued by the National Park Service (NPS) addresses solicitation requirements and revisions for portable toilet delivery, servicing, emergency placement, removal, and related waste-management services at Zion National Park.

Key changes clarify that septic cleaning, ADA-accessible toilets, and handwashing stations are not required; standard units must have a minimum 60-gallon capacity and display the servicing company’s name. All waste must be transported off-site because the Park provides no disposal facility or water source, although unit locations are accessible 24 hours per day.

The existing portable units are owned and serviced by the incumbent contractor, Wayne A. Levitz, under Contract 140P1222P0018. New units may be staged at locations up to two days before January 1 without creating a service gap.

The revised Monthly Price Schedule corrects fiscal-year labels and aligns weekly service frequencies with monthly service quantities for FY2027 through FY2031.

Locations showing zero units must be priced based on whether a unit was previously present: a first-time zero is priced at $0, while removal of an existing unit requires a removal fee. Evaluation will use the schedule’s “Total Evaluated Price.”

Emergency services and emergency placements are included in the evaluated price at the estimated quantities shown, with prices entered per unit. Revised Statement of Work Section 5 and CLINs 0011–0051 establish these emergency requirements as funded, not-to-exceed CLINs rather than options.

For example, an emergency placement requires delivery within 24 hours of notification.

The Questions and Answers document also confirms a $9 million size standard for NAICS 562991, establishes a Zion National Park site visit for November 12, 2026, and extends the deadline for submitting questions to November 18, 2026, by close of business without specifying a particular time.

Subcontracting is generally permitted if the prime contractor demonstrates compliance with FAR 52.219-14 limitations on subcontracting, but the prime remains responsible for contract requirements.

Insurance under DOI clause 1452.228-70 applies to the prime contractor, must be demonstrated after award, is not an evaluation factor, and generally cannot be satisfied solely through subcontractor coverage.
