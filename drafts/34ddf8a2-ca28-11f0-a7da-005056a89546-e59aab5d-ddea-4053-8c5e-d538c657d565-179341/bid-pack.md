Summary: The Septemberfest Committee agenda requires review and action on festival contract matters, 2027 event planning, and committee reports at the November 2, 2026 meeting, scheduled for 7:00 p.m. in Meeting Rooms A and B at the Al Larson Prairie Arts Center, 201 Schaumburg Court, Schaumburg, Illinois.

Key expenditure business continues discussions on the generators and light towers contract and the drone show contract. The generators and light towers issue was previously deferred because permanent onsite power changed the number of generators required and because of a disputed 2026 invoice.

The drone show decision was deferred because of negative public feedback. The committee will also consider the October 5, 2026 minutes, a student-representative scholarship photo with the Sons and Daughters of Italy in America, the 2027 meeting calendar, operating dates and hours, and 2027 fees.

The incorporated October 5 minutes report that contracts for the beer and malt beverage provider/sponsor, carnival and novelty vendor, Main Stage production, private security, tables, and LED screens expired in 2026 and were expected to be solicited through requests for quotes, bids, proposals, or professional services beginning in November.

Electrical services, generators and light towers, sanitation, and temporary labor agreements remain valid through 2027, subject to extension approval. Chair and tent rental contracts remain valid through 2028, while shuttle bus and drone show contracts remain valid through 2029.

The committee approved extensions for the applicable contracts while excluding generators and light towers and the drone show. It also directed that the two tents required on either side of the Main Stage be included in the Main Stage production contract because of required tent-height specifications.
