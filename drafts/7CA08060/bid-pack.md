### Description

Contractor agrees to provide to the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection (CAL FIRE) Portable Restroom and Handwash Station Rental Services, as described herein: Contractor shall provide all materials, labor, equipment, tools, permits, taxes, transportation, supervision, permits, licenses, insurance, and fees for portable restrooms and handwashing stations rental, servicing, maintenance, pumping, cleaning, relocation, and waste disposal services for the CAL FIRE Atwater Training Center.

### HigherGov AI summary

Background
 The California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection (CAL FIRE) seeks rental and servicing of portable restrooms and handwashing stations for its Atwater Training Center. CAL FIRE may adjust quantities and add or remove service locations.

Work Details
 - Provide and maintain up to 18 portable restroom units and 8 handwashing stations. The rate sheet estimates these quantities for 156 weeks over the three-year term; quantities are not guaranteed.
- Provide rental, weekly or requested servicing, maintenance, pumping, cleaning, relocation, and lawful waste disposal. Each service includes pumping, cleaning and sanitizing units, restocking consumables, and minor repairs.
- Units must be clean, sanitary, operational, structurally sound, and compliant with applicable Cal/OSHA, public health, and environmental requirements. Restrooms must be stocked and free of visible waste, leaks, damage, and inoperable doors or locks. Handwashing stations must have working water, soap, and towels.
- CAL FIRE designates unit placements, which may change for training operations. Relocations must be completed within two business days of notification at no additional cost. Respond to routine service requests within two business days, urgent sanitation issues within 24 hours, and emergency health or safety issues the same business day.
- Units must be available 24 hours per day, 7 days per week. Routine service access is Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., excluding State holidays, unless otherwise coordinated. Coordinate delivery and service with CAL FIRE, protect training operations, and dispose of waste offsite.

Period of Performance
 Estimated term: December 1, 2026, or upon California Department of General Services approval, whichever is later, through November 30, 2029. Work may not begin before approval and CAL FIRE’s notice to proceed.

Place of Performance
 CAL FIRE Atwater Training Center, Atwater, California. CAL FIRE may add or remove service locations.

Bidder Requirements
 - Bidder must be an individual or firm licensed to do business in California and hold a valid, active Health Permit to Operate for a Septic Tank/Chemical Toilet Cleaning Business issued by the Merced County Division of Environmental Health. California corporations must be registered and active/in good standing with the California Secretary of State. An out-of-state prime bidder must provide home-state business license or incorporation documents showing good standing.
- Awardee must maintain commercial general liability coverage of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence; workers’ compensation with waiver of subrogation in favor of the State and employer’s liability of at least $1,000,000; and commercial automobile coverage for owned, hired, and non-owned vehicles of at least $1,000,000. The State must be included as additional insured where specified.
- Subcontractors must hold all licenses, permits, and certifications required for their work. A motor carrier permit is required if applicable under California law.
- No DVBE participation goal is required. DVBE participation is encouraged and may qualify for an evaluation incentive of 1% to 5%, based on verified participation, subject to a combined incentive/preference cap of $100,000. Certified California SB/MB bidders may claim a 5% preference, capped at $50,000. A non-small-business bidder may claim the 5% preference by subcontracting at least 25% of its net bid price to one or more certified SBs.
