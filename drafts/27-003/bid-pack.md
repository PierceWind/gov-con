### Description

The City of Beaverton is seeking a qualified operator to manage the Beaverton Safe Parking Program. Safe Parking offers a legal option for people who are experiencing homelessness and living in a vehicle to park and stay in one of five safe parking locations throughout the city while receiving case management from highly qualified professionals with the goal of transitioning to stable housing. Safe Parking consists of a public-private partnership in which the City contracts out with private organizations who have agreed to offer a limited number of parking places on their properties as designated Safe Parking locations.The Request for Proposals (RFP) will award $58,000 for program case management and an additional $15,000 to be used as flexible funds for other expenses (gas cards, minor vehicle repairs, etc.). The City of Beaverton provides additional funds for program infrastructure (port-a-potties, handwashing stations, and storage pods).Qualified applicants will have a strong ability to work with extremely vulnerable populations through a trauma-informed lens while responsibly working in collaboration with the City of Beaverton (including police), other service providers, host sites, surrounding neighbors, and other key stakeholders.

### HigherGov AI summary

Background
 The City of Beaverton is seeking a qualified operator to manage the Beaverton Safe Parking Program. This program provides a legal option for individuals experiencing homelessness and living in vehicles to park and stay at designated locations while receiving case management aimed at transitioning them to stable housing.

The initiative is part of a public-private partnership where the City collaborates with private organizations to offer safe parking spaces. The program aims to support vulnerable populations through trauma-informed care and collaboration with various stakeholders, including police and service providers.

Work Details
 The contractor will provide management services for the Safe Parking Program, which includes:
- Operating the site as a free safe parking program
- Enrolling participants
- Assigning parking spaces
- Maintaining participant rosters
- Adopting and enforcing participant rules
- Providing contact information for staff
- Maintaining cleanliness and safety at the site
- Connecting participants with social services
- Responding to neighbor complaints.

Incident reporting within 24 hours is required for any significant events. Monthly reports detailing participant statistics and program outcomes must be submitted to the City. An annual report summarizing program demographics and success stories is also required. The contractor will establish a system for on-call crisis intervention services.

Period of Performance
 The contract is expected to commence on July 1, 2027, and will end on or before June 30, 2032. There may be options for renewal for up to two additional one-year terms.

Place of Performance
 The services will be performed at five designated safe parking locations throughout the City of Beaverton.

Bidder Requirements
 Proposers must comply with Oregon's civil rights laws prohibiting discrimination in employment practices. They are required to certify their non-discrimination compliance and identify their resident status as defined in Oregon law.

Additionally, proposers are encouraged to include COBID-certified enterprises in their proposals to meet the City's goal of 15% participation from such firms.
