### Description

The town plans to assume responsibility for providing portable restrooms at Unity Park and American Legion Park beginning in calendar year 2027. Town Manager Austin Sigos proposed a $2,000 increase in culture and recreation expenses for this purpose as part of the FY2027 budget adjustments.

This creates a potential portable-restroom rental and servicing opportunity for the two municipal parks. The presentation did not specify the number of units, servicing frequency, seasonal coverage or vendor.

Summary: The virtual town warrant presentation explains Articles 1 through 4 for Sutton’s fall annual town meeting scheduled for October 19, 2026, with doors opening at 6:30 p.m. and the meeting beginning at 7:00 p.m.

Article 1 proposes a $318,334 adjustment to the FY2027 budget adopted on May 11, 2026. The additional revenue consists of $218,334 in certified new-growth tax levy revenue and more than $113,000 in additional state aid.

Proposed expenditures include:

- $100,000 for Sutton Public Schools
- More than $169,000 to backfill a police lieutenant position
- $30,000 for municipal facilities repairs and maintenance
- $15,000 for tax-title expenses
- $6,000 for municipal human-resources costs associated with implementing a time-and-attendance system
- $2,000 for portable restrooms at Unity Park and American Legion Park

Article 2 requests a $250,000 appropriation from an estimated $2.5 million in certified free cash to Sutton’s other post-employment benefits (OPEB) trust fund, consistent with the Select Board’s policy requiring a 10% contribution when free cash exceeds $2 million.

The town’s June 30, 2025, actuarial valuation identified approximately $28 million in OPEB liability. The trust held approximately $6.5 million at that date, leaving a net liability of roughly $21 million and a funding ratio of about 23%.

Article 3 proposes depositing approximately $100,000 into the special-education stabilization fund, representing net FY2026 Medicaid reimbursements after administrative costs.

The fund currently contains slightly more than $400,000 and would approach $500,000 after the contribution, within the Select Board’s target range of $350,000 to $1 million.

Article 4 is a standard prior-year-bills placeholder. No unpaid prior-fiscal-year invoices are anticipated for submission at the time of the presentation.
