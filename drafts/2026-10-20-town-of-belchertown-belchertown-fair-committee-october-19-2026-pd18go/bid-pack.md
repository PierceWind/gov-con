Summary: The Belchertown Fair Committee meeting reviewed the 2026 fair’s outcomes, operational issues, finances, marketing, and preparations for the 2027 season and an upcoming fair convention.

Members reported that severe rain, wind, mud, and poor weather reduced attendance, particularly for animal activities, and created difficult decisions regarding the Saturday-night hay toss. The parade was considered successful.

The revised parking and one-way traffic arrangement substantially improved vehicle flow and allowed parents to drop off participants without congestion. However, members identified safety and coordination issues involving candy being dropped near children, parade gaps, slow-moving units, and participants riding in the back of a pickup truck.

Approximately 69–72 parade entries were scheduled, with only five or six non-band participants failing to appear. Animal and pony classes were smaller than desired.

Financial and participation figures included:

- $25,000 in vendor fees from 12 youth booths, seven adult nonprofit vendors, and 45 adult commercial vendors
- More than $15,000 in sponsorship receipts
- $350 raised through the quilt
- $186 in donated premiums
- 191 exhibitors
- 699 exhibits

Eco Oil collected 175 gallons of used cooking oil. Substantial fair merchandise remains, especially shirts and sweatshirts, and the committee will explore selling the inventory online through Square with pickup at Ace.

Premium payments were still being processed, with parade premiums not yet mailed. Members were asked to submit remaining bills, receipts, petty cash requests, and hay-related expenses to Cindy or Carla.

A reimbursement report for Friends of the Fair was prepared in Excel, while the committee continued clarifying the town’s release and retainer requirements.

Planned follow-up includes investigating whether premium amounts should be increased by comparing fairs that pay awards ranging from $3/$2/$1 to $8/$6/$4. The committee will also determine whether a Belchertown Fair Instagram account can be authorized and linked to Facebook to reach younger audiences.

Future parade-participant guidance will address candy distribution and spacing. The committee submitted entries to the Big E media awards in the poster, brochure, premium book, advertising/souvenirs, and website categories.

Members are awaiting convention pricing for registration, lunch, and dinner. They requested a display table and began assembling raffle-basket items, including fair T-shirts, a sweatshirt, tote bags, and maple syrup.
