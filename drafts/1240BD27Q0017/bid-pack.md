The solicitation requires vendors to submit monthly and total pricing for each line item and option year, along with vendor identification information.

PROJECT
Delivery, installation, and weekly servicing of ADA-accessible portable toilets at recreation sites
SCOPE OF WORK
The Mt. Baker-Snoqualmie National Forest has a requirement for ADA-accessible portable toilets to be
delivered, installed and serviced at the Marblemount and Sauk boat launches. All serviced weekly.
Services include: once a week pump out toilet tank/vault. Replace with clean blue type liquid ready for a
week’s use. Spray down interior, remove trash, and clean/sanitize. Re-stock toilet paper and hand
sanitizer. The Contractor shall furnish all labor, materials, equipment, tools, transportation, and supplies.

Baker Ranger District within Skagit County. See attached map listing Sauk River Boat Launch
and Marblemount Boat Launch:
