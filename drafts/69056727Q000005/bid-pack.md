Background
 The Western Federal Lands Highway Division (WFLHD), part of the US Department of Transportation – Federal Highway Administration, is responsible for designing and administering road construction projects on Federal Lands across several states including Alaska, Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Montana, and Wyoming. 

This solicitation aims to support WFLHD operations during the renovation of their Division Office Facility by acquiring modular buildings and restroom facilities on a temporary lease basis.

Work Details
 The contractor is required to provide the following services: 
1. Lease one prefabricated modular office building (24' x 60') with at least three offices, one small secure meeting room, and two conference rooms that comply with local building codes. 
2. Lease two single-wide container/modular office units (8' x 40') with full HVAC systems and commercial finishes. 
3. Provide one climate-controlled executive restroom trailer with a minimum of three stations. 
4. Manage transport of all units to the WFLHD facility in Vancouver, WA. 
5. Conduct site preparation including grading and leveling for unit placement. 
6. Perform installation of all units including utility connections to the nearest WFLHD building. 
7. Conduct post-installation inspections and adjustments within 30-60 days after occupancy to ensure compliance and stability. 
8. Provide ongoing maintenance services including weekly septic pumping, freshwater refills, winterization assistance, and troubleshooting support.

Period of Performance
 The work is expected to be completed over a period of twenty-four months starting approximately from February 1, 2027, to January 31, 2029.

Place of Performance
 The contract will be performed at the WFLHD secure federal facility located at 610 East Fifth Street, Vancouver, Clark County, WA 98661-3801.

Bidder Requirements
 Bidders must demonstrate relevant experience in providing similar services within the last seven years and must submit documentation including technical specifications and performance information for evaluation. The contractor must also comply with local commercial building codes and ensure that all products provided are sustainable as required by the contract.

SECTION D – STATEMENT OF WORK
1.	BACKGROUND
Western Federal Lands Highway Division (WFLHD), a division of the US Department of
Transportation – Federal Highway Administration, designs and administers, primarily,
road construction projects on Federal Lands in the states of Alaska, Washington, Oregon,
Idaho, Montana, and Wyoming.
2.	OBJECTIVE
The objective of this requirement is to support and maintain WFLHD operations during the
Division Office Facility Renovation by obtaining prefabricated modular office building(s),
single-wide container/modular office units, and an executive restroom trailer on a
temporary lease basis, including professional delivery, site preparation, installation, utility
connection, preventative maintenance, weekly septic pumping maintenance, technical
troubleshooting support, and eventual teardown/demobilization.
3.	LOCATION
The delivery and service location shall be at the WFLHD secure federal facility located at
610 East Fifth Street, Vancouver, Clark County, WA 98661-3801.
The Contractor must coordinate the delivery schedule, truck access, and exact site
placements with the Technical Point of Contact (TPOC) at least 10 business days prior to
on-site delivery. This coordination is critical to ensure that delivery vehicles can access
the compound and that drop zones are clear of obstruction. The Contractor is responsible
for preparing the sites, offloading, setup, and placing/setting all units.
4.	PERFORMANCE  PERIOD
It is anticipated that the work listed in this order shall be completed over a period of twenty-
four (24) months beginning approximately 01-Feb-2027 and ending approximately 31-Jan-
2029
.

small secure meeting room, and two conference rooms, fully compliant with local
commercial building codes.
● Procure and supply two 8' x 40' Office Containers or Modular units (fully finished,
with active HVAC systems and full office finishes).
● Procure and supply one modern, climate-controlled executive restroom trailer (3-
station minimum).
