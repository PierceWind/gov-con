### HigherGov AI summary

Background
 The California Highway Patrol (CHP) is procuring portable luxury restroom trailer rental and maintenance services to provide restroom facilities for its Marin Area office.

Work Details
 - Rent and maintain two trailers: one three-stall unit with one ADA stall and one sink per stall, and one two-stall unit with one sink per stall.
- Clean and pump both trailers three times per week. Cleaning includes sanitizing interior and exterior surfaces and fixtures with EPA-approved disinfectants and bio-enzymatic drain cleaners. Provide the chemicals and deodorizer needed to keep the units clean and serviceable.
- Dispose of pumped waste at a county- or city-approved landfill in accordance with applicable laws, and remove service debris. Provide a wheelchair-accessible unit at each location in compliance with UFAS; equipment must meet current ADA codes and regulations.
- Supply all necessary equipment, labor, personnel, and supervision. Bid rates must include delivery and pickup.
- Miscellaneous repairs are excluded from this agreement and would be pursued separately. Repair or replace, at no cost to CHP, damage the contractor causes to state property or damage to or loss of the toilets while on state property.
- Guarantee workmanship and materials for two years from acceptance.

Period of Performance
 Anticipated term: December 1, 2026, through November 30, 2028. Services commence December 1, 2026, or upon approval, whichever is later. Services are scheduled Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., excluding State holidays.

Place of Performance
 CHP Marin Area office, Corte Madera, California.

Bidder Requirements
 - By the bid due date, bidders must hold a valid business license, a City/County-issued County Environmental Health Permit, and a Disposal Site Discharge Permit. A Department of Motor Vehicles Motor Carrier Permit is required for vehicles with a GVWR of 10,001 pounds or more.
- The contractor and listed subcontractors must be registered with DIR before bidding; the contractor must maintain registration throughout the agreement. The work is public work, subject to prevailing wage requirements and DIR compliance monitoring and enforcement.
- Corporations must certify that they are in good standing and qualified to do business in California; nonprofit organizations must certify eligibility for nonprofit status. Bidders must have a record of sound business integrity and responsiveness to prior contractual obligations, and provide three references for similar services performed in the past five years.
- Before agreement execution, the awarded contractor must provide liability insurance meeting Exhibit E requirements. Assigned personnel are subject to driver-license checks before facility access; fingerprint checks may also be required. CHP may require personnel replacement or cancel the contract based on adverse findings.
- DVBE participation is waived and not mandatory. Certified DVBE participation may qualify bidders for an incentive. Optional state preferences include a small/micro-business preference and a non-small-business preference for bidders committing at least 25% of the bid to certified small-business subcontractors performing commercially useful functions.
