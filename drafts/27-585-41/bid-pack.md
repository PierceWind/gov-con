### HigherGov AI summary

Background
 The Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources (DNR) is seeking temporary porta potties for facilities within the State of Wisconsin. The goal of this contract is to provide standard-sized and handicap accessible porta potties at various DNR managed locations such as boat launches and parks. This contract aims to ensure that visitors have access to sanitary facilities while enjoying outdoor activities.

Work Details
 The contractor will provide standard-sized porta potties and handicap accessible porta potties. Each standard-sized unit must accommodate up to 10 workers for a 40-hour work week, which also applies to the handicap accessible units. The contractor is responsible for pumping, servicing, and cleaning all porta potties as needed, ensuring compliance with industry standards and local regulations. A service schedule must be provided to the DNR for each location serviced. Delivery, removal, travel, and fuel costs are considered incidental and will not incur separate charges.

Period of Performance
 The initial contract term is three years, with the possibility of two one-year extensions upon mutual agreement between the contractor and the DNR.

Place of Performance
 The services will be performed at various locations throughout the State of Wisconsin, specifically at DNR managed facilities including parks and boat launches.

Bidder Requirements
 Bidders must meet mandatory qualifications including providing completed Attachment C verifying years of experience in the industry, completed Attachment B, and completed DOA-3832 form. Bidders are encouraged to utilize minority-owned business enterprises (MBEs), veteran-owned businesses (VBs), disabled veteran-owned businesses (DVBs), and woman-owned business enterprises (WBEs) in their proposals.
