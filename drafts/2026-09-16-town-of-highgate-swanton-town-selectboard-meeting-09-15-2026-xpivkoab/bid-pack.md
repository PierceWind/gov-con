They proposed a gate and fencing or landscaping barriers, noted that camera footage is retained for one month, and identified tree branches obstructing camera views and one nonfunctioning camera. Public works reported that the truck clutch and branch-chipper clutch had been repaired, the loader returned to service, roadside mowing and shoulder-material work were continuing, and winter sand hauling would begin soon.
