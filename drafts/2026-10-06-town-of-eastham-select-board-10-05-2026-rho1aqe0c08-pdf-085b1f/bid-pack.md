### Description

Cape Cod National Seashore staff reported that the oil supply line used to heat the Salt Pond Visitor Center satellite restroom has failed: “We need to do a contract for that work.” The satellite restroom will close starting December 1, 2026. Notices will be posted beginning November 1 to give regular visitors time to make alternative arrangements.

Staff will put two portable toilets at the location during the closure because the facility is described as the only public restroom open during winter on the Outer Cape. The identified requirements include contracted repair of the failed heating-oil supply line and provision of temporary restroom facilities; portable-toilet rental and servicing may be associated needs. No repair completion date or contract value was stated.

Summary: The Select Board meeting covers Town of Eastham governance matters and provides a Cape Cod National Seashore operational update, including visitation, staffing, facility repairs, erosion response, capital projects, transportation planning, wildfire management, and coordination with the town.

Board members reminded residents that the November 3 election ballot contains 11 questions across two pages, with Questions 10 and 11 specifically addressing Eastham water and wastewater funding and system operations.

The Board also announced proposed Board of Health regulation hearings scheduled for November 19, excluding wastewater matters, and January 28 for wastewater regulations, after which the Board of Health may vote on implementation.

National Seashore officials reported that January–August 2026 visitation increased 9.4%, with more than four million annual visits anticipated, while 90 seasonal employees supported the summer season and 52 permanent employees remained on staff against 14 critical vacancies.

Eastham facilities and services—including beaches, trails, bike paths, parking areas, lifeguards, trams, law enforcement, fee collection, custodial services, and interpretation—operated fully during the season.

The Salt Pond Visitor Center satellite restroom will close beginning December 1 because of a failed oil-heating supply line, with two portable toilets provided.

Potable water at the Nauset Light Beach bathhouse remains pending Massachusetts Department of Environmental Protection review of well-testing results and is hoped for by summer 2027.

The update detailed completed and planned projects, including:

- Replacement of the Button Bush Trail boardwalk
- Demolition of the Bartlett House after bluff erosion
- Historic wallpaper restoration at the Penniman House
- Storm cleanup following the blizzard
- Repairs to the Three Sisters lighthouses
- Improvements to the Button Bush Children’s Discovery Area
- Painting at Coast Guard Beach and the Penniman barn
- A 10-year Nauset Light Preservation Society lease covering the keeper’s house

Officials stated that Eastham’s ocean bluff and beach are retreating an average of 12–30 feet annually, with approximately 60 feet lost in five years and 160 feet in 40 years.

At Nauset Light Beach, the park will remove five parking spaces and alter traffic flow when the bluff reaches five feet from designated monitoring points.

Two new tram front cars are expected in 2027 to support transportation from Little Creek.

The Cable Road multi-use path has resumed detailed design under an awarded contract, with construction potentially beginning in fall 2027 after surveys, tree removal, and protected-species restrictions are addressed.

The National Park Service also reported that fire personnel have shifted to the newly created U.S. Wildland Fire Service, leaving the seashore without dedicated fire staff but continuing coordination with regional firefighters and Eastham emergency departments.

The Federal Advisory Commission is expected to meet November 2, 2026, subject to Federal Register notice.
