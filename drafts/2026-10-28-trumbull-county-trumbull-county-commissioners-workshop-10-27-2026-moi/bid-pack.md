Summary: The Trumbull County Commissioners’ workshop agenda and discussion address routine approvals, budget actions, emergency-management projects, transportation matters, and upcoming procurement activity.

The commissioners considered approval of prior workshop and regular-meeting recordings as official minutes, payment of bills, additional appropriations, and transfers of already-budgeted funds. Additional appropriations included:

- $6,000 for Emergency Management Agency seminars and training
- $100,000 for Family and Children First Council needs and social services
- Two $5,000 Planning Commission interfund reimbursements
- $500 for Transportation Improvement District audit fees

The board also received documentation supporting designation of Someplace Safe to receive 2027 domestic-violence shelter funding derived from marriage-license and divorce fees.

EMA officials presented a $17,809 change order for the EMA remodeling project, covering replacement soffits and gutters, roof-related work, and extensive electrical cleanup required after unsafe legacy wiring was discovered.

Despite the change order, the project’s final cost was reported to remain approximately $10,000 below the June projection. The board also reviewed a mutual-aid memorandum of understanding with Mahoning County concerning its SWAT team and discussed whether future use of Trumbull County’s nearly $1 million mobile command center and related personnel should include reimbursement for labor, time, and equipment wear.

The EMA building dedication and open house were announced for Thursday, with the dedication at 2:00 p.m. and public access afterward until 7:00 p.m.

Highway and engineering matters included standard special-hauling, road-weight, and right-of-way permits.

Officials discussed approval planning for Genesee Elm Road intersection improvements in Warren, scheduled for bidding in fall 2026 and construction beginning in spring 2027, with 80% federal Congestion Mitigation and Air Quality funding through Eastgate.

They also provided updates on bridge and roadway projects expected to open to traffic in November or December while final work continues into 2027.

Officials discussed uncertainty caused by flooding and closure of Morton Salt’s Fairport mine. Trumbull County anticipates using approximately 28,000 tons of salt for the 2026–2027 season, has about half that amount stored, and estimates current supplies should cover November through January.

The county ordered an additional 3,000 tons despite limited storage, with possible tarping at approximately $3 per ton, and is coordinating with ODOT and other suppliers.

The sheriff’s office and engineering staff also discussed replacing an approximately 15-year-old truck used for weight and hauling enforcement, while expressing concern over possible delays in monthly state gas-tax distributions of roughly $350,000–$400,000.

The Planning Commission requested authorization to advertise public notice for the Rural Water Facilities Project, funded through approved Community Development Block Grant allocations.

Bid documents were scheduled to become available from the Planning Commission beginning Monday, November 2, 2026, with bids due to the commissioners’ office by Friday, November 20, 2026, at 2:00 p.m.
