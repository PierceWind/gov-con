### Description

Bethany Parks & Recreation is planning a new trail race using Veterans Park and adjoining land trust trails. Mike stated that he would meet with Bob from the land trust the following week to walk a proposed course and determine feasibility. The concept is a four-mile loop, with an eight-mile option consisting of two loops. An initial turnout of 50 to 100 runners would be considered a success, primarily high-school-age participants and adults.

Once the course is mapped, Mike intends to begin contacting timing companies: "I want to get a few quotes" because companies charge different prices per bib. Another commissioner stated, "We should get three quotes anyways with anything." The envisioned service includes chipped race bibs that distinguish one-loop and two-loop participants and record the correct start and finish times. Staff discussed obtaining timing-company referrals from other local races and recreation departments; no supplier was selected.

Related event purchases discussed include participant T-shirts, awards, and portable toilets. Possible awards include medals for age groups and small items such as pint glasses for the top three male and female finishers. Officials also identified aid stations, volunteers, course marking, emergency-service requirements, waivers, marketing, and a race budget as planning needs. The land trust's anticipated role would include providing trail access, helping mark the course, and supplying volunteers to guide runners.

May 16, 2027 was initially proposed, but officials also discussed moving the event to fall 2027, potentially September or October. No date was finalized and no action was taken. Planning will return to the November meeting. The original spring schedule contemplated marketing and registration preparation by January 1, with commission review followed by Board of Selectmen approval, but the schedule may change if the event moves to fall.
