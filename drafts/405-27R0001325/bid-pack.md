### HigherGov AI summary

Background
 The Department of Public Safety (DPS) of the State of Texas is seeking bids from qualified contractors to supply, install, and maintain a mobile restroom facility at the DPS Livingston location. The goal of this contract is to ensure adequate restroom facilities are available for personnel and visitors at the specified site.

Work Details
 The contractor must furnish, install, and maintain a mobile restroom facility that includes:

1) One two-stall non-ADA mobile restroom facility equipped with:
   a) 110 Volt AC/20 amp electrical power;
   b) Air conditioning for heating and cooling;
   c) Water supply via a standard garden hose spigot or an onboard water storage tank of at least 125 gallons;
   d) An external water storage tank of at least 300 gallons with an auxiliary pump;
   e) An onboard waste water holding tank of at least 350 gallons;
   f) Stall entry doors/platforms with stairs and handrails;
   g) One female stall with a sink and flushing toilet;
   h) One male stall with a sink, flushing toilet, and wall-mounted urinal;
   i) Sinks equipped with hot and cold running water;
   j) Additional amenities in each stall including sink vanity, mirror, interior lighting, paper towel dispenser, and hand soap dispenser.

The unit must be serviced bi-weekly by the contractor to refill water tanks, pump out waste tanks according to state regulations, disinfect all surfaces, and clean floors.

Period of Performance
 The initial service period will commence on the date of award and continue through August 31, 2027. There are four optional one-year renewal periods available.

Place of Performance
 The services will be performed at the Department of Public Safety located at 3161 US-59, Livingston, TX 77351.

Bidder Requirements
 Bidders must comply with all requirements outlined in the solicitation including submission of a completed Subcontracting Plan (SP), if applicable. Bidders are encouraged to demonstrate good faith efforts to recruit certified Historically Underutilized Businesses (HUBs). Additionally, bidders must provide evidence of financial stability upon request.
