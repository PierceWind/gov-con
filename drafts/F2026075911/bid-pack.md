### Description

The overall objective of this project is to provide the United States Secret Service at the James J. Rowley Training Center with turnkey, temporary facilities that expand and sustain its law enforcement training capabilities. The Contractor shall furnish, install, maintain, and remove a climate-controlled temporary fitness facility supporting strength, cardiovascular, and functional conditioning; an adjacent temporary shoot house facility; and accessible restroom and shower trailers. The project includes all required site preparation, utilities, power generation and distribution, lighting, life-safety systems, equipment options, and post-removal site restoration necessary to deliver safe, functional, and fully supported temporary training environments in accordance with Government-approved plans.
