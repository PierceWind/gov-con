1. Executive Summary 
Temporary 123 Inc. is pleased to submit this bid in response to IFB No. 11213 for the rental, deployment, 
servicing, and maintenance of climate-controlled portable restroom trailers for the City and County of 
Denver. Our company specializes in providing turnkey temporary sanitation and support infrastructure 
solutions for municipalities, emergency management agencies, disaster response operations, public 
events, wildfire base camps, temporary sheltering operations, and federal agencies throughout the United 
States. 
With extensive experience supporting government and emergency-response contracts, Temporary 123 has 
successfully deployed restroom trailers and related temporary facilities for large-scale operations 
involving hundreds of occupants in both urban and remote environments. Our operational experience 
includes emergency shelter support, wildfire incident base camps, disaster recovery missions, public 
events, military support services, and seasonal surge operations requiring rapid mobilization and 
continuous servicing under demanding conditions. 
Temporary 123 maintains the personnel, transportation assets, vendor partnerships, and operational 
systems necessary to provide rapid deployment and ongoing support throughout the Denver metropolitan 
area. We understand the critical importance of sanitation services in emergency sheltering and public 
support operations and are prepared to deliver fully operational restroom trailer units within the timelines 
established in the solicitation. Our team is capable of coordinating multiple simultaneous deployments 
and scaling services as operational requirements evolve throughout the contract term. 
Our restroom trailer fleet is specifically equipped to support all-season operations, including winter and 
freezing-weather conditions as required under the Scope of Work. Units are equipped with climate control 
systems, freeze-protection measures, heated interiors, and utility configurations designed to maintain safe 
and sanitary operation during Colorado winter conditions. Temporary 123 also provides comprehensive 
servicing support including waste collection and disposal, cleaning and sanitization, pump-out services, 
consumable replenishment, transportation, and emergency maintenance response. 
Temporary 123 recognizes the City’s requirement for responsive service and operational reliability. We 
are committed to maintaining rapid response capabilities for service calls, wastewater removal requests, 
emergency repairs, and replacement units when necessary. Our operations team is available to coordinate 
scheduling, dispatch, maintenance support, and communication throughout the duration of the contract to 
ensure uninterrupted service and compliance with all local, state, and federal requirements. 
In addition, Temporary 123 supports environmentally responsible operating practices through the use of 
compliant cleaning products, proper waste handling procedures, preventive maintenance programs, and 
operational practices designed to minimize environmental impact while maintaining safe and sanitary 
conditions for all users. 
We appreciate the opportunity to submit this proposal and look forward to partnering with the City and 
County of Denver to provide dependable, high-quality restroom trailer rental and servicing solutions in 
support of its sheltering and public assistance operations.

2. Company Overview 
Temporary 123 Inc. is a nationwide provider of premium temporary sanitation and infrastructure support 
services specializing in luxury restroom and shower trailer rentals for government, municipal, emergency 
response, disaster recovery, and public event operations. Our company provides turnkey deployment, 
servicing, maintenance, and operational support for temporary facilities designed to deliver safe, sanitary, 
and comfortable environments for users in both planned and emergency settings. 
Temporary 123 is also a GSA Contract Holder, demonstrating our experience working 
with federal agencies and our ability to meet government contracting standards for 
pricing, operational performance, compliance, and service delivery. Our experience 
supporting the government has enabled us to develop scalable deployment capabilities 
and responsive service operations suitable for municipal sheltering and public assistance environments. 
For IFB No. 11213, Temporary 123 is pleased to offer premium luxury 3-in-1 combination restroom 
and shower trailers capable of supporting the City and County of Denver’s summer and winter sheltering 
operations. Our available fleet includes both 3-stall and 8-stall trailer configurations, allowing the City 
flexibility to scale operations based on occupancy demands and shelter locations. 
Unlike standard portable restroom units, our trailers are modern, professionally maintained, and 
specifically designed to provide a significantly higher level of comfort, cleanliness, accessibility, and user 
experience. Units feature well-lighted interiors and exteriors, climate-controlled environments, modern 
finishes, flushing toilets, integrated handwashing stations, durable flooring, ventilation systems, and 
private shower accommodations. The combination restroom and shower functionality provides an 
efficient all-in-one sanitation solution that is particularly valuable for emergency sheltering and extended 
occupancy operations. 
Our restroom trailers are fully equipped for year-round deployment, including winter and 
freezing-weather operations required under the solicitation. Units include heating systems, insulated 
components, freeze protection systems, and operational configurations designed to maintain reliable 
performance during Colorado winter conditions. These features allow the trailers to safely support shelter 
residents, staff, and emergency personnel during both summer and cold-weather response operations. 
Temporary 123 maintains the operational capability to support deployments serving 200–500 users per 
day through scalable fleet availability, responsive servicing operations, and coordinated logistics support. 
Our experienced operations and dispatch teams oversee transportation, setup, servicing schedules, 
emergency maintenance response, consumable replenishment, and wastewater removal to ensure

The company has experience supporting municipal agencies, emergency management operations, disaster
