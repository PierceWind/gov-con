1. Company Overview 
Temporary 123, Inc. is a proven national provider of temporary life-support infrastructure, including 
berthing, shower, and restroom trailers, specializing in emergency response, basecamp operations, and 
mission-critical support services for military, federal, state, and local agencies. 
Since 1998, we have built a strong reputation for rapid, compliant deployments tailored to support 
operational continuity in austere and active-duty environments. Our team has successfully delivered 
services supporting wildfire response, military exercises, disaster relief operations, and large-scale 
mobilizations across the country. 
Temporary 123 operates under an active GSA Multiple Award Schedule (MAS 
#47QSMS25D00AM), ensuring our offerings meet federal procurement standards 
for quality, safety, and price competitiveness. For this RFQ, our past performance 
includes multiple deployments of HUD-certified modular housing units and fully 
serviced sanitation trailers, all engineered to meet or exceed Coast Guard and DoD facility 
requirements. 
Each unit is: 
●​ Built to meet federal fire safety, electrical, and mechanical codes

installations

With an active SAM registration, Temporary 123 maintains the readiness to deploy assets within 24–72 
hours, ensuring full support for urgent or scheduled mission needs. We are fully prepared to meet the 
Coast Guard’s technical and scheduling requirements at Station Lake Worth Inlet.

Temporary 123, Inc. proposes a turnkey solution to deliver, install, and maintain temporary berthing, 
restroom, and shower trailer systems to support the operational readiness of USCG Station Lake Worth 
Inlet. Drawing on over two decades of experience supporting military, emergency, and disaster response 
deployments, our technical approach prioritizes operational continuity, crew welfare, regulatory 
compliance, and responsiveness. 
Our solution will meet or exceed the requirements outlined in the solicitation and Statement of Work 
(SOW) by providing:

durability, sanitary operation, and efficient servicing. 
●​ Full lifecycle support services, including transportation, utility hook-up, servicing, and final 
demobilization. 
Temporary 123, Inc. is fully committed to meeting the U.S. Coast Guard’s operational timelines and

applicable environmental and safety regulations. In addition, trailer interiors will be thoroughly 
sanitized, all consumable supplies (including toilet paper, soap, and related items) will be

4. Equipment Specifications 
Sleeper/Quarters/Berthing Trailers 
​
Temporary 123’s Sleeper/Quarters Trailers are purpose-built to provide exceptional comfort, privacy, 
and safety in temporary housing environments, far exceeding the industry standard of open-bay or 
communal sleeping layouts. Designed to support up to 16 personnel, each unit features private or 
semi-private rooms equipped with individual locking doors, lighting, power outlets, and controlled 
ventilation, creating a secure and restful environment that enhances wellness, morale, and 
recovery—especially critical for personnel operating under high-stress conditions. 
Our trailers are fully climate-controlled, using integrated HVAC systems with digital thermostats to 
ensure occupant comfort in all weather conditions. They are constructed using fire-rated materials and 
insulated with Rockwool, providing superior thermal regulation and sound attenuation. Ingress and egress 
points are compliant with all applicable fire codes, and ADA-ready configurations are available upon 
request to ensure accessibility for all personnel. 
These berthing units are HUD-certified and designed to operate reliably in rugged or remote deployment 
environments. For space-constrained sites, units are available with stackable modular designs, 
maximizing footprint efficiency without sacrificing comfort or safety. Every aspect of these trailers is 
engineered to support sustained field operations and align with the mission-readiness goals of the U.S. 
Coast Guard. 
 
Restroom with Shower Trailers 
Temporary 123’s Restroom and Shower Trailers are purpose-engineered to deliver a hygienic, private, and 
durable sanitation solution in high-demand field environments. These units are built for continuous, 
high-volume use, supporting the operational needs of personnel stationed at remote or active-duty sites. 
Each trailer includes separate compartments for restrooms and showers, featuring private, 
single-occupancy stalls with individual locking doors and independent HVAC ventilation to ensure
